Built around you

The HARDKOR Diet is fundamentally different because it starts with lean body mass. Two athletes can weigh the same, carry different amounts of muscle, and need different amounts of food. Lean body mass helps account for that difference.

Your daily schedule, training, and activity help determine how much food you need. Your plan turns that calculation into how many servings to eat each day and exactly how much food counts as one serving.

  • No calorie counting
  • No macro math
  • No guessing at portions
  • Choose foods from your list.
  • Eat the servings in your plan.
  • Fit them into your day.
